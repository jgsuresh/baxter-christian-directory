# Glossary of shifted-meaning words

Words in Baxter that look familiar but meant something different (or
broader) in 1673. For each: what he means, and what we do. "Kept" means the
word stays in the modernized text and this entry is its standing note.

| Baxter's word | What he means | Our treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **melancholy** | A lasting low, fearful, or brooding state, understood as partly rooted in the body. Overlaps with, but is not the same as, modern depression or anxiety. | Kept. |
| **flesh, fleshly** | Not just the body: the sinful or worldly side of human appetites and desires, as in Paul's "flesh vs. Spirit." | Kept. |
| **sensual, sensuality** | Of the senses and bodily appetites generally (food, drink, comfort, sex), not only sexual. | *physical*; *indulgence in physical pleasure*. |
| **carnal** | Unspiritual; ruled by the flesh. | Kept, noted at first use. |
| **conversation** | Conduct, way of life, not only talk. | *conduct* or *way of life* when he means behavior. |
| **discourse** | Talk. | *conversation* or *talk*. |
| **affections** | Emotions and desires: love, fear, joy, anger, etc. | Kept, since it is a technical term for him. |
| **pretended** | Alleged, claimed. | *supposed* or *alleged*. |
| **vain, vanity** | Empty, worthless, pointless. Not conceited. | *empty*, *worthless*; *empty pursuits*. |
| **sports** | Amusements, entertainments, pastimes. | *amusements*. |
| **covetousness** | Love of money and possessions, broadly; greed. | *greed*. |
| **delight** | A central term: settled, deep gladness in something, a mark of the believer's relation to God. | Kept. |
| **habit (of body)** | A settled physical condition or constitution. | *bodily constitution*. |
| **passionate** | Swayed by strong feeling. | *emotional*. |
| **act of oblivion** | An amnesty: a legal pardon that "forgets" offenses. | *amnesty*. |
| **prevent** | Go before, anticipate (as in "prevenient grace"). | Look at each case; usually *go before*. |
| **suffer** | Allow. | *allow*. |
| **convince** | Prove wrong, refute. | *prove wrong* or *show*. |
| **religion** | Worship and devotion, practiced piety. | Kept, noted if it could mislead. |

New entries are added whenever a round of modernizing meets a word that
needs a standing decision.

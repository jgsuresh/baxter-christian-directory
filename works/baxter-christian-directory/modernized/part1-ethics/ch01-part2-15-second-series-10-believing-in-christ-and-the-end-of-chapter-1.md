# Chapter 1, Part 2: Second Series, Temptation 10 (the last of Chapter 1)

**Temptation 10 (second series).** But, says the tempter, it is not reigning, unpardoned sin, because you believe in Jesus Christ; and all that believe are pardoned, and justified from all their sin.

**Direction 10.** He that savingly believes in Christ takes him entirely for his Savior and Governor; and gives himself up to be saved, sanctified, and ruled by him. As trusting your physician implies that you take his medicines, and follow his advice, and so trust him; and not that you trust to be cured while you disobey him, by mere trusting: so it is as to your faith and trust in Christ; it is a belief or trust that he will save all those that are ruled by him in order to salvation. Christ "became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him" (Hebrews 5:9). If you believe in Christ, you believe Christ: and if you believe Christ, you believe "that except a man is converted, and born again, he cannot enter into the kingdom of heaven" (John 3:3, 5; Matthew 18:3); and that he that is in Christ "is a new creation; what is old has passed away—look, what is new has come!" (2 Corinthians 5:17); and that without holiness "no one will see the Lord" (Hebrews 12:14); and that "no fornicator, effeminate person, thief, greedy person, drunkard, reviler, extortioner, murderer, or liar shall enter into, or have any inheritance in, the kingdom of Christ" (1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:4–6; Revelation 21:27; 22:14–15). If you believe Christ, you must believe that you cannot be saved unless you are converted. It is the devil, and not Christ, that tells you that you may be pardoned and saved in an unholy, unregenerate state: and it is a sorry thing that men should believe the devil, and call this a believing in Christ, and expect to be saved for so believing; as if false faith and presumption pleased God! Christ will not save men for believing a lie, and believing the father of lies above him; nor will he save all that are confident they shall be saved. If you think you have any part in Christ, remember Romans 8:9, "if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, this person does not belong to him." [45]

## Translator's notes

- **Scripture translation:** NET Bible®; see [NOTICE.md](../../../../NOTICE.md).
- **Hebrews 5:9:** Baxter's "He is the author of eternal salvation to all them that obey him" is "he became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him" in the NET.
- **John 3:3, 5; Matthew 18:3** (Direction 10): the quotation "that except a man be converted, and born again, he cannot enter into the kingdom of heaven" is Baxter's blend of John 3 and Matthew 18, as in Direction 2 of Part 1. We kept his wording, modernized, and cited both.
- **2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 12:14; Romans 8:9:** NET wording, as in earlier sections.
- **1 Corinthians 6:9–10** (Direction 10): Baxter's list ("no fornicator, effeminate, thieves, covetous, drunkards, revilers, extortioners, murderers, liars …") is his adaptation of 1 Corinthians 6:9–10 together with Ephesians 5:4–6 and Revelation 21:27 and 22:15. He adds "murderers" and "liars," which are in Revelation but not in 1 Corinthians 6. We kept his list, modernized, and cited all the passages he gives. **"effeminate"** is the King James rendering of a Greek word in 1 Corinthians 6:9 that translators disagree about. The NET reads "passive homosexual partners, practicing homosexuals" for that phrase; for the others it reads "the sexually immoral, idolaters, adulterers … thieves, the greedy, drunkards, the verbally abusive, and swindlers." We kept Baxter's own word and flag it so you can decide whether to keep it or replace it.
- **Ephesians 5:4–6; Revelation 21:27; 22:14–15** are cited only.
- **mere trusting:** Baxter's "bare trusting." **expect to be saved:** his "think to be saved." **above him:** his "before him." **a sorry thing:** his "sad."
- **greedy person:** Baxter's "covetous." **thief; extortioner:** left in singular for the list.
- **savingly believes:** left as written; it means believes in a way that saves.
- **"the father of lies"** (Direction 10): the devil, from John 8:44. **"If you believe in Christ, you believe Christ"**: left as written; Baxter means that believing in someone includes believing what he says.
- **This is the last section of Chapter 1.** Chapter 1 contains the Introduction to Part 1, the 20 Directions of Part 1, and the 30 plus 10 Temptations and Directions of Part 2. Baxter's chapter notes [10]–[45] have each been reproduced in the section where its marker stands.

# Chapter 1, Part 2 (continued): Temptations 18–22

**Temptation 18.** Another temptation is to draw the sinner on into such a custom in sin, and long neglect of the means of his recovery, until his heart is utterly hardened.

**Direction 18.** Against this, read later, chapter 4, part 2, against hardness of heart.

**Temptation 19.** Another temptation is to delay repentance, and resolve to do it later.

**Direction 19.** Of this I ask you to read the many reasons which I have given to shame and awaken delayers, in my book of *Directions for a Sound Conversion*.

**Temptation 20.** The worst of all is to tempt them to flat unbelief of Scripture and the life to come.

**Direction 20.** Against this, read here chapter 3, Direction 1, chapter 4, part 1, and my *Treatise against Infidelity*.

**Temptation 21.** If they insist on looking for grace, he will do all he can to deceive them with counterfeits, and make them take an apparent half conversion for a saving change.

**Direction 21.** Of this read my *Directions for Sound Conversion*, and the *Formal Hypocrite*, and *Saints' Rest*, part 3, chapter 10.

**Temptation 22.** If he cannot make them flat unbelievers, he will tempt them to question and contradict the meaning of all those texts of Scripture which are used to convince them, and all those doctrines which grate most upon their sore consciences; such as the necessity of regeneration, the fewness of those that are saved, the difficulty of salvation, the torments of hell, the necessity of mortification, and the sinfulness of all particular sins. They will listen to what quibblers can say for any sin, and against any part of godliness; and with this they willfully deceive themselves.

**Direction 22.** But if men are resolved to join with the devil, and shut their eyes, and quibble against all that God says to them to prevent their misery, and do not know because they will not know; what remedy is left, or who can save men against their wills? "Now this is the basis for judging: that the light has come into the world and people loved the darkness rather than the light because their deeds were evil. For everyone who does evil deeds hates the light and does not come to the light, so that their deeds will not be exposed" (John 3:19–20). In Scripture "some things ... are hard to understand, things the ignorant and unstable twist to their own destruction" (2 Peter 3:16). Of particulars read the end of my *Treatise of Conversion*.

## Baxter's notes

None in these five pairs.

## Translator's notes

- **Scripture translation:** NET Bible®; see [NOTICE.md](../../../../NOTICE.md).
- **John 3:19–20:** NET wording, as in Direction 9 of Part 1. The King James has "this is the condemnation"; the NET has "this is the basis for judging." Baxter's "lest his deeds should be reproved" is "so that their deeds will not be exposed."
- **2 Peter 3:16:** the NET reads "Some things in these letters are hard to understand, things the ignorant and unstable twist to their own destruction, as they also do to the rest of the scriptures." Baxter quotes it as a statement about Scripture in general ("In Scripture, some things are hard to be understood …"). In the verse, "these letters" are Paul's letters. We leave out "in these letters" with an ellipsis, as Baxter's wording generalizes the point. Flagged.
- **These five pairs are mostly cross-references.** Baxter's Directions 18, 19, 20 and 21 only point to other chapters of this book or to other books of his: chapter 4 against hardness of heart; *Directions for a Sound Conversion*; chapter 3, Direction 1, chapter 4, part 1, and his *Treatise against Infidelity*; and *Directions for Sound Conversion*, *The Vain Religion of the Formal Hypocrite*, and *Saints' Rest*, part 3, chapter 10. We have not checked these sections yet.
- **unbelievers:** Baxter's "infidels." **sore:** his "galled." **quibblers:** his "cavillers." **listen:** his "hearken." **deceive:** his "delude." **awaken:** his "waken."
- **insist on looking for grace:** Baxter's "will needs look after grace." **apparent half conversion:** his "seeming half conversion."
- **mortification** (Temptation 22): kept in its theological sense of putting to death one's sinful desires.
- **grate upon their sore consciences:** the meaning is that these doctrines rub painfully on consciences that are already sore. Left as written.
- **delayers:** people who put off repentance. Kept as Baxter's word.
- **"Of particulars read the end of my *Treatise of Conversion*"** (Direction 22): Baxter's pointer to a fuller treatment of each of the doctrines listed in Temptation 22. Kept.
